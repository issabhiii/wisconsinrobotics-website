Subteam Operations

Powering the organization behind Wisconsin Robotics

What We Do

The Operations subteam is the backbone of Wisconsin Robotics, handling everything from event coordination and social media to industry relations and logistics. We ensure the team runs smoothly, maintains strong community engagement, and secures the resources needed for success.

Events & Outreach

Planning and executing events, demos, and community outreach programs to inspire the next generation of engineers and roboticists.

Media & Communications

Managing our social media presence, creating engaging content, and telling the story of Wisconsin Robotics to the world.

Industry Relations

Building partnerships with sponsors, securing funding, and maintaining relationships with industry leaders and alumni.
